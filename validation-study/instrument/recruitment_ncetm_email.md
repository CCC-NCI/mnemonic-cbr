# Email to NCETM (National Centre for Excellence in the Teaching of Mathematics, UK)

**To:** `info@ncetm.org.uk` (or via the contact form at `https://www.ncetm.org.uk/contact-us/`; verify the current research-call route on the NCETM site)
**Subject:** Request: research recruitment via the Maths Hubs network — rating tutoring dialogues

---

Dear NCETM team,

I am writing from the National College of Ireland (Dublin) to ask whether NCETM, or one or more of the regional Maths Hubs, would consider circulating a short research recruitment call to your teacher network through your newsletter, a Maths Hubs bulletin, or a single hub lead's distribution list.

The study is a methodology paper on how human readers and a software rating system score short tutoring dialogues about secondary-level maths. The recruitment target is around 25 maths-aware readers (UK secondary or US middle-school maths comfort) who would each rate 15 short dialogues on two pedagogical criteria. The task takes 15 to 20 minutes including two practice trials with feedback, runs entirely in a browser, and is pseudonymous: only a random 8-character participant code is recorded with the ratings; no name, email, or IP address is captured in the research data. The full protocol is being deposited as a timestamped pre-registration on the Open Science Framework before the data are analysed.

The study has National College of Ireland Research Ethics Committee approval for the methodology; an amendment naming the mailing-list and Prolific recruitment channels is in process with the committee.

The recruitment text I would like circulated, in the form a member would see, is below:

> Do students actually learn anything in short tutoring dialogues?
>
> I'm Dietmar Janetzko at the National College of Ireland in Dublin. I'm running a short research study on how readers rate tutoring dialogues for evidence of student progress, and I'm looking for ~25 maths-aware readers (UK secondary level or equivalent). The task takes 15 to 20 minutes: you read 15 short student-teacher exchanges about secondary maths and give each one two simple 1-to-5 ratings. Two practice trials with feedback first.
>
> A worked example with the expected scores is shown on the welcome screen so you can decide whether the rating logic feels right for you before continuing.
>
> Pseudonymous: random 8-character code, no name, email, or IP recorded in the research data. Unpaid.
>
> https://tutorial-dialogue-rating.netlify.app/
>
> Many thanks &mdash; Dietmar Janetzko, NCI

A note on equity across recruitment channels: I am also running a small paid recruitment round on Prolific to ensure the study reaches its statistical target. NCETM and Maths Hub recruits would do the same task unpaid, as a contribution to research on AI in maths education. I am happy to acknowledge NCETM's help in the final manuscript, and to share the de-identified aggregate results once the study completes.

If circulating the call is something NCETM can do, or if you would prefer to route it to a single Maths Hub lead rather than the national network, please let me know. I am also happy to provide further detail on the methodology, the ethics application reference, or the OSF pre-registration once deposited.

Many thanks for considering this.

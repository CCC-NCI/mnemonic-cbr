# Email to IMTA (Irish Mathematics Teachers Association)

**To:** `hello@imta.ie` (verify on `https://imta.ie` before sending)
**Subject:** Request: 15-minute research call for IMTA members — rating tutoring dialogues

---

Dear IMTA committee,

I am writing from the National College of Ireland (Dublin) to ask whether you would consider forwarding a short research recruitment call to IMTA members through your newsletter or members' mailing list.

The study is a methodology paper on whether and how students learn math in tutorial dialogues. I am looking for around 25 maths-aware readers (Leaving Certificate Ordinary Level Mathematics at about O3–O4) to rate 15 short dialogues on two pedagogical criteria. The task takes 15 to 20 minutes including two practice trials with feedback, runs entirely in a browser, and is pseudonymous (random participant code; no name, email, or IP captured in the research data). The full protocol is being deposited as a timestamped pre-registration on the Open Science Framework before the data are analysed.

The recruitment text I would like circulated, in the form a member would see, is below:

> Do students actually learn anything in short tutoring dialogues?
>
> I'm Dietmar Janetzko at the National College of Ireland in Dublin. I'm running a short research study on how readers rate tutoring dialogues for evidence of student progress, and I'm looking for ~25 IMTA members or maths-aware readers. The task takes 15 to 20 minutes: you read 15 short student-teacher exchanges about maths (Leaving Certificate Ordinary Level Mathematics at about O3–O4) and give each one two simple 1-to-5 ratings. Two practice trials with feedback first.
>
> A worked example with the expected scores is shown on the welcome screen so you can decide whether the rating logic feels right for you before continuing.
>
> Pseudonymous: random 8-character code, no name, email, or IP recorded in the research data. Unpaid.
>
> https://tutorial-dialogue-rating.netlify.app/
>
> Many thanks &mdash; Dietmar Janetzko, NCI

I am happy to acknowledge IMTA's help in the final manuscript.

If the call is something IMTA can circulate, or if you would like any wording revised, please let me know. I am also happy to provide further detail on the methodology, the ethics application reference, or the OSF pre-registration once deposited.

Many thanks for considering this.

# Email to colleagues

**Subject:** Quick favour — 10 minutes on a questionnaire pre-test

---

Hi [name],

I'm running a small study on how people read short tutoring dialogues between a maths teacher and a student, and I'd love your take on the questionnaire before I send it out to actual study participants.

Attached is a single HTML file. Open it in any browser — no software to install, no account to create, no data sent anywhere automatically. You'll get a welcome page, the rubric I'm asking participants to use, two short practice trials with feedback so the rubric makes sense in concrete cases, five tutoring dialogues to score, and a comments box at the end. About 10 minutes start to finish.

When you reach the end, the questionnaire opens your mail client with your results pre-filled in the body. You just hit send and the results come back to me. (There's also a copy-paste fallback box in case your default mail client misbehaves.)

One important thing: there's a worked example in the welcome screen so you can see the kind of maths I'll be asking you to read. **If the maths does not feel comfortable, please drop out at that point.** It is honestly more useful to me than guesswork later, and it costs nothing — just close the tab.

Thanks for the time. Any comments at all — confusing wording, things that did not work, things that did — are useful at the comments box at the end.

Dietmar
